Pearson's correlation measures the strength of the relationship between two continuous variables. The formula for calculating it is:
$$
r = \frac{\sum(x_{i}-\bar{x})(y_{i}-\bar{y})}{\sqrt{ \sum(x_{i}-\bar{x})^2 \cdot \sum(y_{i}-\bar{y})^2}}
$$

Alternatively, it can be written as
$$
r = \frac{cov(x, y)}{\sigma_{x}\sigma_{y}}
$$

Its range is between -1 and 1.
