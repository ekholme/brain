Deliberate practice is a focused approach to learning that targets explicit weaknesses and works to develop skills/knowledge in those areas. It's a more effective way to develop a skill than through simple "passive" repetition (naive practice).

Some of the core components of deliberate practice include:
- **Narrow Goals:** during a given practice session, you should set and pursue specific, narrow goals. Rather than practicing "basketball," you might practice dribbling with your offhand while running.
- **Immediate & Actionable Feedback:** you need a way to detect and fix errors before bad habits crystalize.
- **High Mental Investment:** deliberate practice requires high mental investment (largely due to the self-monitoring component). A corollary of this is that it's not possible to engagement in deliberate practice for marathon sessions.
